# First-time setup: do these in this order (as the owner)

1. **Create the owner login** (once, from your computer — see `docs/ENV_VARIABLES.md` step 10), then sign in on the website.
2. **Offices & villages** (left menu → *Offices & villages*): create your head office, then each village office. Open an office → **Add village**.
   The village *code* (e.g. `KHT`) becomes the start of every customer ID in that village (`KHT001`, `KHT002`…).
3. **Packages** (*Packages* → New package): name, speed, price. All packages last 30 days.
4. **Settings** (*Settings*, optional): installation fee, whether it is billed on the first bill or separately, and the financial-year start month.
5. **Employees** (*Employees* → New employee): username, temporary password, role and office. The role gives default permissions;
   use **Permissions** to adjust them per office. Give each person their temporary password; they must change it at first sign-in.
6. **Customers**: register them one by one (*Customers → New customer*), or import your old data (*Data import*: dry run first).
7. **Pilot**: use ONE office for a few days and compare with your paper registers before adding the others.

The dashboard shows a "Getting started" checklist for the owner until offices, villages, packages and employees exist.
If a menu item is missing, that person does not have the permission for it: the owner can change this under *Employees → Permissions*.
